Adaptations for this demo: selected 770 full-body OBJ components without cropping
the head, limbs, feet or skin; changed coordinates/scale; simplified meshes;
assigned ivory/red/blue and organ/skin materials; grouped major vessel and organ
segments; added bilingual labels, default visibility/opacity and structure IDs.
The full-body model contains 640 selectable structures: 277 skeletal, 230 arterial,
116 venous, 16 organ and one complete skin structure. Lung parenchyma uses 18
real 4.3 components FJ6595-FJ6612, while Skin is the native 4.0 FJ2810 mesh.
V2 preserves this core GLB byte-for-byte and all 640 IDs/defaults. Four verified
core ligaments are reclassified only in metadata. Added lazy assets are:
muscles.glb: 413 real structures; nerves.glb: 65; connective.glb: 26 new structures.
The connective layer additionally contains four original-core ligaments. Tissue
metadata distinguishes muscle, tendon, ligament and fascia; spinal-cord neural
tissue is central and is not labeled as a peripheral nerve. Every added GLB uses
the same original coordinate rotation, scale and center as the unchanged core.
Ten 3.0 named muscles supplement absent 4.0/4.3 anatomy after bounded native
coordinate checks; version surface differences remain, with no independent fitting.
These GLB derivatives retain the historical CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan attribution and
ShareAlike license. Source URLs, versions, geometry hashes and limits are recorded
per structure in asset-audit.json and ASSETS.md.
The anatomy.glb derivative and this project's model modifications are distributed
